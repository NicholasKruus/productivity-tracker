    How these numbers are counted, and what's missing
    • Where they come from. A script on your Mac writes small files to a Google Drive folder every hour, a daily Claude routine fills a log sheet, and Gmail and Slack are read live. This page reads them all when it opens.
    • Published counts items in the public feeds you list: YouTube channels and any RSS or Atom feed, such as a Substack or a blog. Each item counts once, on the day it appeared.
    • Commits come from every git repo in your home folder up to three levels down, except Desktop, Documents and Downloads, which macOS guards. Only commits under your own emails count, so Claude's commits in your repos do and cloned tools don't.
    • Conversations are judged daily by a Claude routine from your meeting notes and calendar. It counts talks about work, career or your field with people outside your organization, and talks about your field with colleagues. It never counts classes, health appointments, family or purely social plans, and it skips any folders you mark private.
    • Applications come from receipts in your Gmail and applications you emailed. One with no receipt or email is missed.
    • Emails are your sent mail in Gmail over the last 12 weeks. An email whose recipients are all unsubscribe, bounce or no-reply addresses isn't counted, and neither is one only to yourself. Gmail lists at most five messages per thread, so a very long thread can be undercounted. Slack messages are the ones Slack search finds from you.
    • Distracting time is everything RescueTime rates distracting or very distracting. Social media is the categories you choose, such as General Social Networking. RescueTime is read once a day, up to yesterday.
    • Claude's work per prompt is hours Claude worked divided by prompts you wrote, per week, for weeks with at least 5 prompts. Commits or posts per Claude hour would measure output more directly, but much of Claude's work (admin, research, setup) produces neither.
    • Typed to Claude counts the words in Claude Code prompts you wrote, minus pastes, code blocks and system text. Dictated counts superwhisper words. A resumed Claude Code session copies the earlier conversation into a new file, so each entry counts once.
    • Hours Claude worked runs from each prompt you send to Claude's last step before your next one. The clock stops after 15 idle minutes. Sessions running at once each count. Claude Code deletes transcripts after 30 days, so the script keeps each day's counts once they're 20 days old.
